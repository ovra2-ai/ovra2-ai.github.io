Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

OVRA2 is a personal video player for the Mac. It runs on your Mac. It has no servers of its own, so the people who make it never receive your files, your account details or your viewing history.

Google Drive

If you connect a Google account, OVRA2 uses Google's permission to:

  • see your name and your Google account's ID number, so it can show which account is connected;
  • list the video files in the folders you choose, with their names, sizes and checksums;
  • stream those videos to your Mac when you play them;
  • move a video to that account's Google Drive Trash, only when you choose to remove it or turn on automatic cleanup of watched episodes, which is off by default.

OVRA2 does not change your videos and does not use your Google data for advertising. It does not sell it or use it to train AI.

What stays on your Mac

Your sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Your library lists, watch history, settings and artwork are kept in files on your Mac.

What is sent to other services

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): a title taken from the file name, to find posters, artwork and details.
  • OpenSubtitles: a title, only when you search for subtitles.
  • Microsoft OneDrive and Google Drive: the requests needed to reach your own files in the accounts you connect.

These services have their own privacy policies. OVRA2 contains no analytics or advertising.

Removing your data

You can disconnect an account in OVRA2's Settings, and you can remove OVRA2's access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Deleting OVRA2 and its files from your Mac removes what it stored there.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for OVRA2.
